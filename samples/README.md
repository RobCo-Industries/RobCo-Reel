# samples

Small tapes that show how to use Reel and the Pip-OS SDK. Each one builds and runs in the QEMU tests.

## What lives here

- `hello/`: a tape that links Reel, logs one line with Reel's version and exits with status 0. It proves the build chain and nothing more.

The small game of plan milestone S6 and Culvert, after S8, come later.

## What must never live here

- Any file under a licence other than MIT. People copy samples into their own tapes.
- A sample without a test that boots it.
- Art, text or sound copied from the Fallout games.
